PRE-PRODUCTION NOTES
====================
Resolution: 1080p — smooth playback and clean motion are the priority, not raw resolution
Frame rate: 60fps for screen recordings, 24fps for cinematic B-roll and Veo clips
Color grade: Warm shadows, cool highlights — matches the app's dark-mode UI palette
Music: Ambient electronic underscore, 60 BPM, builds through demo, resolves softly at close
VO: Clear, measured, ~130 WPM. No rushed delivery — let visuals breathe between lines
Transitions: Match-cuts and soft dissolves (8 frames) throughout. One intentional smash-cut
             at problem peak. Zero hard jump-cuts elsewhere.

SOURCE RULE: Every app screen shown must be from the screenshots listed above.
             Veo is used only for B-roll (people, environments). Never generate UI with Veo.
